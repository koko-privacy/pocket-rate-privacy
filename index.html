Pocket Rate（口袋汇率）隐私政策

本政策说明 Kokoship（本应用的开发者，以下简称“我们”）在提供 Pocket Rate（口袋汇率，以下简称“本应用”）时如何处理信息，以及您可以如何联系我们。本政策适用于本应用目前的汇率查询与多币种换算功能。

简要说明：本应用无需注册或登录。金额换算在您的设备上完成；输入金额不会由本应用发送给我们或汇率服务提供方。币种列表、语言偏好和汇率缓存保存在设备上。获取汇率时，本应用会连接第三方服务，该服务会收到完成网络请求所需的信息，包括 IP 地址。

1. 输入金额与本地数据

  • 输入金额：仅用于设备上的换算计算，本应用不将金额保存到持久化存储，也不将其加入汇率请求。切换页面时，当前金额可能继续保留在本次运行的内存中。
  • 币种列表与顺序：保存在设备上，用于在再次打开应用时恢复您的选择。
  • 语言偏好：保存在设备上，用于恢复您选择的界面语言。
  • 汇率缓存与更新时间：保存在设备上，用于减少重复请求，并在离线时提供已保存的参考汇率。

这些本地信息不会由本应用上传到我们运营的服务器。设备操作系统可能根据其备份设置备份或恢复部分应用数据；此类处理由相应系统和您的设置控制。

2. 汇率服务与网络请求

本应用使用 ExchangeRate-API 提供的参考汇率，通过 HTTPS 从 open.er-api.com 获取数据。请求使用固定的汇率接口，不包含您输入的金额、选择的币种列表、语言偏好或应用用户账号。

汇率服务及其基础设施会接收到您的 IP 地址，以及请求时间、请求地址和可能由网络客户端自动发送的技术信息。这些信息可能用于提供服务、安全防护、限流及服务日志。第三方对这些信息的处理、保存期限及处理地点由其自身政策决定；您可访问其网站查看相关隐私说明。

我们不出售您的个人信息，也不通过本应用将输入金额或本地偏好分享给广告商。当前版本不包含广告、行为分析或专用崩溃报告 SDK，也不创建用于追踪您的应用用户标识。

3. 应用权限与外部链接

核心功能需要网络连接与应用本地存储，不需要您提供通讯录、精确位置、相机或麦克风数据。若操作系统显示权限信息，请以您实际安装版本的权限清单为准。

点击汇率来源、隐私政策或其他外部链接时，您可能进入浏览器或第三方网站；这些网站的信息处理受其各自隐私政策约束。打开本政策网页时，网页托管服务也可能接收 IP 地址及常规访问日志。本政策页面不设置分析脚本或广告追踪脚本。通过 GitHub Pages 托管时，可参阅 GitHub 隐私声明。

4. 联系我们时提供的信息

如果您通过邮件联系我们，我们会收到您的邮箱地址，以及您主动提供的邮件正文和附件。这些信息仅用于处理您的问题、回复请求以及必要的支持与安全管理，不用于广告营销。请勿发送密码、支付凭据或其他非必要的敏感信息。

支持邮件会在处理请求和必要的后续跟进期间保留；在不再需要时删除，法律义务或处理争议所需的保留除外。邮件服务提供方也会按其政策处理邮件。

5. 保存期限与删除

本地偏好通常会保留至您清除应用数据；汇率缓存会在获取新数据时更新。您可以在 Android 系统设置中清除本应用的存储数据，或卸载应用来移除其本地数据；操作系统备份中的副本需依据系统备份设置管理。清除数据后，重新打开应用会恢复默认设置，并可能重新下载汇率。

本应用没有用户账户，因此无需办理账户注销。如果您希望删除此前通过邮件向我们提供的信息，请使用下方邮箱联系我们。对于第三方汇率服务独立保留的请求日志，请根据该服务的政策联系其处理。

6. 安全与跨地区处理

汇率请求使用 HTTPS。本地数据的保护取决于设备与操作系统的安全措施；任何网络传输或存储方式均不能保证绝对安全。第三方汇率、邮件或网页托管服务可能在您所在国家或地区以外处理网络请求或支持信息。

7. 儿童隐私

本应用提供通用汇率工具，不提供面向儿童的账户或社交功能，也不会主动要求儿童提交姓名、联系方式或其他个人信息。如您认为儿童通过支持邮件向我们提供了不必要的个人信息，请联系我们，以便采取适当措施。

8. 政策更新

若应用功能、第三方服务或信息处理方式发生变化，我们会更新本政策及页面顶部的更新日期。重大变化将通过适当方式告知。本政策不替代第三方服务自身的隐私政策。

Pocket Rate Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Kokoship (the developer of this app, “we”) handles information when providing Pocket Rate (“the app”) and how you can contact us. It covers the app’s current exchange-rate lookup and multi-currency conversion features.

In brief: No registration or sign-in is required. Conversions take place on your device, and the app does not send entered amounts to us or to the exchange-rate provider. Currency selections, language preferences, and cached rates are stored on your device. When fetching rates, the app connects to a third-party service that receives information needed for the network request, including your IP address.

1. Entered amounts and local data

  • Entered amounts: Used for on-device calculations. The app does not save them to persistent storage or include them in rate requests. The current amount may remain in memory during the app session when switching screens.
  • Currency list and order: Stored on your device to restore your selections when you reopen the app.
  • Language preference: Stored on your device to restore your selected interface language.
  • Cached rates and update times: Stored on your device to reduce repeated requests and make saved reference rates available offline.

The app does not upload this local information to servers operated by us. Your operating system may back up or restore some app data according to its backup settings; this is controlled by the relevant system and your settings.

2. Exchange-rate service and network requests

The app uses reference rates provided by ExchangeRate-API, fetching data over HTTPS from open.er-api.com. Requests use a fixed rate endpoint and do not include entered amounts, your selected currency list, your language preference, or an app user account.

The rate service and its infrastructure receive your IP address, request time, request address, and technical information that the network client may send automatically. This information may be used for service delivery, security, rate limiting, and service logs. The third party determines its processing practices, retention periods, and processing locations under its own policies. Please visit its website for its privacy information.

We do not sell your personal information or use the app to share entered amounts or local preferences with advertisers. The current version does not include advertising, behavioral analytics, or dedicated crash-reporting SDKs, and it does not create an app user identifier to track you.

3. App permissions and external links

The core features use network access and local app storage; they do not require you to provide contacts, precise location, camera, or microphone data. If your operating system displays permission information, refer to the permission list for the version you actually install.

Opening a rate-source link, privacy policy, or another external link may take you to a browser or third-party website, whose information handling is governed by its own policy. When you open this policy webpage, the hosting service may also receive your IP address and routine access logs. This page does not set up analytics or advertising tracking scripts. When hosted on GitHub Pages, see the GitHub Privacy Statement.

4. Information you provide when contacting us

If you email us, we receive your email address and any message content or attachments you choose to provide. We use this information to handle your issue, respond to your request, and carry out necessary support and security administration, not for advertising marketing. Please do not send passwords, payment credentials, or unnecessary sensitive information.

Support emails are retained while needed to resolve your request and provide necessary follow-up, then deleted when no longer needed, except where retention is required for legal obligations or disputes. The email provider also handles messages under its own policies.

5. Retention and deletion

Local preferences generally remain until you clear the app’s data; cached rates are updated when new rates are fetched. You can clear the app’s storage in Android settings or uninstall the app to remove its local data. Manage any operating-system backup copies through your system’s backup settings. Reopening the app after clearing data restores defaults and may download rates again.

The app has no user accounts, so no account-deletion process is needed. To request deletion of information you previously sent us by email, contact the address below. For request logs independently retained by the third-party rate service, contact that service according to its policies.

6. Security and processing locations

Rate requests use HTTPS. Protection of local data depends on your device and operating system’s security measures. No transmission or storage method can guarantee absolute security. Third-party rate, email, and hosting services may process network requests or support information outside your country or region.

7. Children’s privacy

The app provides a general-purpose exchange-rate tool. It has no child-specific accounts or social features and does not actively ask children to provide names, contact details, or other personal information. If you believe a child has sent unnecessary personal information through support email, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

8. Policy updates

We will update this policy and the date at the top of the page when app features, third-party services, or information-handling practices change. Material changes will be communicated as appropriate. This policy does not replace third-party services’ own privacy policies.
